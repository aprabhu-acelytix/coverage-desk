Switch to Needs review and All collected to show that unverified dates and unrelated findings were retained. Explain that Refresh performs owner-operated planning, search and assessment; do not click it while the durable allowance is exhausted.

**0:45-1:30 - Evidence.** Open the case study's message evidence. Its missing repair/reuse message does not make the brand coverage irrelevant. Show the query and retrieval observations, then explain that excerpts are not full articles and a model citation alone is not verification.

**1:30-2:15 - Team board.** Open the shared reviewed example. Its implementation-agent validation note is explicitly labeled; it is not a teammate demonstration. Add your own perspective if desired. Private material requires an explicit sharing choice. Teammates cannot invoke the owner's AI.

**2:15-3:00 - Briefings.** Open Patagonia | reviewed research example, edit its title or text, then choose Preview & publish. Confirm the exact source links, content and configured public destination. Stop at the preview unless you intentionally want to publish. Only your final confirmation posts.

After extra allowance is approved, demonstrate one fresh Refresh: Understanding > Searching > Assessing > relevant/review results. A pending-evidence action works across the run, not the current page. Search details names the actual provider path and what was attempted.

Visual checks still required in native Slack: desktop and narrow width, long headlines, light/dark modes, first finding placement, detail-modal spacing, empty/partial/limit states, and a real teammate's own contribution. Payload acceptance and fixture files are not visual or multi-user evidence.
